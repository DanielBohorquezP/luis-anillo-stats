2. Personal Data We Collect

Through the request and contact forms available on this website, the following personal data are collected:

Your personal data will be processed for the following purposes, which you expressly authorize by ticking the consent box on the form:

  • Answering your statistical consulting request — handling the specific inquiry you send through the form.
  • Follow-up contact related to services — contacting you to follow up on your inquiry, offer information about available services or resume conversations already started.

Your personal data is processed on the basis of the express authorization of the data subject, given by ticking the consent box available on the forms, in accordance with Article 9 of Law 1581 of 2012.

5. Rights of the Data Subject (Habeas Data)

As the owner of your personal data, you have the following rights, which you may exercise at any time:

Know Find out what data we hold about you and how we use it.
Update and correct Correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data.
Delete Request deletion of your data when there is no legal obligation to keep it.
Revoke authorization Withdraw the consent given for the processing of your data.
File a complaint Before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, send your request to anilloluis1@gmail.com stating: your full name, the right you wish to exercise and a clear description of your request. We will handle your request within a maximum of 15 business days, in accordance with Article 14 of Law 1581 of 2012.

Your personal data will be kept for as long as there is an active relationship with Luis Ángel Anillo Arrieta (ongoing inquiry, active project or expressed interest in the services) or until you request its deletion.

Once none of the purposes described in section 3 remains, we will securely delete or anonymize your data.
